Buying a five-figure domain should feel boring — in the best way. Here is the exact checklist used for ABQ.Dog acquisitions.

## Step 1 — Agree on terms

Email [sales@desertrich.com](mailto:sales@desertrich.com?subject=Offer%20for%20ABQ.Dog) with your offer amount, timeline, and intended use. You will receive written confirmation of price, what is included (domain + registrar push + transfer assistance), and response within 1–2 business days.

## Step 2 — Open escrow

Funds go to a licensed escrow service (e.g. Escrow.com), never directly to the seller. Verify the escrow instructions independently — never change payment details over an unverified channel.

## Step 3 — Registrar push

The domain is pushed to your account at the same registrar (fastest) or transferred with an authorization code. Confirm the name appears in *your* account before releasing anything.

## Step 4 — Confirm and release

Verify WHOIS/registry control, confirm DNS, then authorize escrow to release funds to the seller. Typical end-to-end timeline: a few days once terms are agreed.

## Red flags to avoid

- Seller refuses escrow or rushes off-platform payment.
- Price changes mid-transfer without written amendment.
- "Free" transfer services that ask for registrar credentials — use push or auth-code flows only.
