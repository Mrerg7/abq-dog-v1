How much is a short city + category domain worth? This guide breaks down the seven factors professional buyers use — and how ABQ.Dog scores on each.

## 1. Exact-match local intent

"ABQ" is how Albuquerque shortens itself in search, on vans, and on social handles. Pairing it with `.dog` captures queries like "dog daycare Albuquerque" in a single memorable brand. Exact-match local domains convert because the ad *is* the address.

## 2. Length and radio test

Seven characters including the dot. It passes the radio test: say "ABQ dot dog" once and anyone can spell it. Every extra character in a brand domain costs recall — short premium domain names hold value for that reason.

## 3. TLD fit

`.dog` is a purpose-built category TLD, not a compromise ccTLD. For a pet brand it reads as intentional, and it sidesteps the six-figure price of the equivalent `.com`.

## 4. Commercial versatility

One name covers daycare, boarding, grooming, and training. Multi-service fit widens the buyer pool beyond a single niche — a key driver for investment domains.

## 5. Comparable sales pattern

Short geo + service domains (city + category) trade on memorability and lead flow rather than type-in traffic alone. Buyers typically underwrite them against 1–3 years of branded-search ad spend they replace.

## 6. Development optionality

ABQ.Dog works as an operating brand *or* a lease-to-own asset. Optionality supports the ask: the domain earns whether you build on it or hold it.

## 7. Transfer cleanliness

Single-owner, escrow-eligible, registrar push included. Clean transfers close faster — see our [secure transfer checklist](/insights/transfer-checklist/).
